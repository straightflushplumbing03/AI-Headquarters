# Brand Voice — Straight Flush Plumbing & Leak Detection

Derived from existing site copy and automation prompts (verified 2026-10-09).
Slogan: **"Always A Safe Bet."**

## Characteristics

- **Honest & no-pressure.** Tell homeowners when a repair is unnecessary or a
  simpler option exists. Reviews (per `llms.txt`) frequently mention no upselling.
- **Educational, first-hand.** Explain *how* something works and *why*, from real
  field experience — not generic marketing copy.
- **Diagnose-first.** Language reinforces "locate before we cut": acoustic,
  electronic, and thermal detection narrow the leak to a small area before any
  concrete, tile, or drywall is opened.
- **Plain language.** Short sentences. Homeowner-friendly terms, technical terms
  defined on first use.
- **Local.** South Orange County, copper-era homes (1970s–1990s), expansive clay
  soils.

## Do

- Use second person ("you", "your home").
- Lead with the direct answer, then explain.
- Name the process steps and what the homeowner should expect.
- Keep phone CTA visible: **(949) 374-6524**.

## Don't

- Don't use superlatives ("best," "#1"), fear tactics, or pressure ("call now or
  else"), or promises the owner has not authorized.
- Don't invent statistics, review counts, or local facts.
- Don't keyword-stuff or repeat the company name unnaturally.
- Don't promise insurance coverage, rankings, or specific outcomes.
