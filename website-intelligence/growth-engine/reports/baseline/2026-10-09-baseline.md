  (full list in `COMPETITOR_DIRECTORY.md`).

  2. **efficientplumbing.com** — general + slab. `/laguna-niguel-plumbers/`.
  3. **barkerandsonsplumbing.com** — general + OC leak + best. `/city/…` pages.
  4. **rotorooter.com** — general + emergency + best. Franchise city pages.
- **Verified advantage (evidence-backed):** city-page **paired with** a specific
  service page (e.g. city page + slab-leak-detection page). SF pairs city/service
  pages less often at the per-city × per-service level.
- **Hypotheses (not yet verified as causal):** exact-match local domains
  (`proplumberlagunaniguel.com`, `lagunaniguelplumber.org`) rank for some queries;
  directory dominance (Yelp/Angi/Thumbtack) for "best" queries.
- **Gaps to address:** (a) per-city × service deep pages for Tier-1 cities;
  (b) dedicated slab-leak pages per Tier-1 city; (c) GBP + review presence.
